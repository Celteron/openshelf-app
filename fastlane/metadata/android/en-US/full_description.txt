<p>OpenShelf keeps track of your physical library: what you are reading, whether you already own a book, and where it stands. No account, no ads, no tracking. The network is only used when you ask for a cover or book details.</p>
<ul>
<li>Library with rooms, shelves and compartments, slipcases, series, tags and lending</li>
<li>Shelf wall with book spines in cover colours, search and drag-and-drop</li>
<li>Scan a barcode and learn at once whether you own the book, which volumes of a series are missing, and keep a shopping list</li>
<li>Reading sessions, goals, notes and quotes, quotes from a photo with offline text recognition</li>
<li>Statistics with your own tiles, a reading calendar and a year in review</li>
<li>CSV and JSON export and import, including Openreads, Goodreads and StoryGraph files</li>
</ul>
<p>OpenShelf is a fork of Openreads and free software under the GPL-2.0.</p>
